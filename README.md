## 🍏 How to Install on iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Open [https://mustafiz-cell.github.io/namaz-reminder/](https://mustafiz-cell.github.io/namaz-reminder/) in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share icon (⬆️)** at the bottom of the screen.
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen" (➕)**.
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner.
5. The app will launch in standalone full-screen mode and works completely offline!
